HOLDOUT · ACCOUNT

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account in the Holdout app:

  1. Open Profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type the confirmation word and choose Delete forever.

This removes your profile, duels, check-ins, social content, and uploaded proof and avatar photos. Purchase records may be retained only where required for tax, fraud-prevention, or legal obligations. Deleting Holdout does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription; manage that subscription in your store account.

Can't access the app?

Email privacy@holdout.hamitcf.info from the address on your account. We will verify ownership before processing the request.
